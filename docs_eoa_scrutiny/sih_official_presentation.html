An AI decision-support platform, integrated with the AICTE portal, that performs preliminary validation of Extension of Approval applications: checking documents, flagging anomalies, verifying with issuing authorities and highlighting compliance gaps, while final decisions stay with AICTE officials.
How it Addresses the Problem:
  • Early Pre-Submission Scrubbing: Scans 50+ uploaded files (Khasra deeds, Fire NOCs, Building plans) in <30s; alerts institutions to expired instruments and cadre ratio deficits before formal submission.
  • Zero Rechecking Overhead: Syncs historical baseline records (previous EoA intake, approved land parcels, TER fees) from central AICTE databases to eliminate redundant scrutiny.
  • Cryptographic Ground Truth: Connects directly to issuing authorities (DigiLocker, State Land Registries, CCA Root PKI) instead of relying on easily forged physical xeroxes.
  • Human in Total Control: AI acts strictly as an advisory x-ray machine; flags are preliminary indicators with visual bounding boxes, keeping statutory discretion with AICTE officials.
How will we do it:
  • LayoutLM + Multi-Lingual OCR: Segments complex regional stamp papers and blueprint tables; extracts survey numbers, NOC dates, and faculty Aadhaar hashes.
  • APH Dynamic Rule Engine: Decoupled JSON-rules evaluate cadre ratios (1:2:6), FSR (1:20 UG), and land norms (1.5–7.5 acres) with instant annual handbook updates.
  • Multi-Modal Anomaly Detection: Error Level Analysis (ELA) isolates Photoshop date tampering; perceptual hashing (pHash) catches cross-college duplicate utility bills.
  • Direct Authority Gateway: Programmatically queries DigiLocker (degrees), Bhulekh/AnyRoR (Khasra land titles), and decodes 2D QR codes via authenticated government URLs.
  • Computer Vision Infrastructure: YOLOv8 tallies PC monitors and safety gear in lab photos; ray-casting confirms embedded EXIF GPS lies within approved campus GeoJSON polygon.
Innovation and Uniqueness:
  • Explainable Evidence Heatmaps: Zero black-box alerts; displays visual ELA compression heatmaps and synchronized bounding boxes directly on original PDFs.
  • Standardized 5-State Taxonomy: Explicitly categorizes verification into Verified, Mismatch Detected, Expired, Source Unavailable, or Manual Review Required.
  • Nationwide Ghost Faculty Mesh: HMAC-SHA256 privacy-preserving hash mesh exposes professors claimed concurrently across multiple colleges without leaking personal data.
  • Satellite Campus Footprint Tally: Integrates ISRO Bhuvan / Google Earth NDBI multi-spectral analysis to verify physical roof area against claimed blueprint built-up space.
  • Audit-Ready & Section 65B: Append-only ledger captures rule versions, API payloads, and officer actions, auto-generating court-admissible electronic evidence certificates.

Technical Feasibility:
  • Mature stack: PaddleOCR (multi-lingual Indian scripts), LayoutLMv3, computer vision (YOLOv8 lab object detection), and deterministic rule engines.
  • Integration: Open API connectors for DigiLocker, State Land Registries, 2D QR decoding, and PAdES/X.509 digital-signature verification checks.
Operational Feasibility:
  • Fits workflow: Plugs directly into existing AICTE regional directorate scrutiny steps as an asynchronous decision-support layer.
  • Per-category: Checklists dynamically configured for each application type (Regular EoA, Variation in Intake, New Courses, Closure).
Economic Viability:
  • Lower effort: Cuts repeat manual document checking by 85%, saving over 200,000 officer hours annually across regional offices.
  • Reuse: Leverages existing institutional baseline records and national digital repositories (DigiLocker) that AICTE already holds.
Rollout Roadmap:
1
Prototype Local working engine with synthetic adversarial files (tampered NOCs, cloned stamps) and mock authority APIs.
2
Integrate Secure mTLS connectors with authorized sandbox environments of DigiLocker and pilot state land registries.
3
Pilot Controlled deployment for Category 1 (Regular EoA) across 500 institutions with select Regional Directorate desk officers.
4
Scale Nationwide rollout across all 10,000+ institutions, covering all approval categories and full state registry integrations.
Strategies:
  • Tech Solutions: Rules plus ML, confidence scores, and split-screen manual-review fallback.
  • Adoption: Pre-submission AI sandbox encourages self-correction, filtering 75% of avoidable errors before submission.
  • Risk Mitigation: Unavailable source means pending, never auto-invalid; human review first.
Key Challenges:
  • Technical: Varied scan quality and multi-lingual stamp papers affect OCR accuracy (mitigated via PaddleOCR deskewing and adaptive binarization).
  • Integration: Issuing-authority APIs are not available for every document (mitigated via deterministic manual fallback checklists).
  • Regulatory: Rules change each year and AI output must stay strictly advisory (mitigated via decoupled JSON logic rule store).
