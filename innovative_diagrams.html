Strategic Value Proposition

Challenges, Smart Solution & The Game-Changer

How our software transforms a 30-year paper bottleneck into automated, transparent national governance.

Challenges in Old Paper Process
⚠️
  • Physical Paper Silos: Lakhs of old physical receipts locked in almirahs across 10 Regional Offices. Searching one receipt takes months.
  • Photoshop & Fake Receipts: Colleges can easily edit amounts or deposit dates on computer printouts without human officers noticing.
  • Silent Expiry Rollover: FDRs mature unnoticed and get rolled over by banks into zero-interest accounts, risking college security deposits.
  • 180-Day Refund Delay: Genuine colleges wait 6+ months for physical verification letters to travel back and forth by postal courier.
Our Smart AICTE Solution
💡
  • AI Key-Value Reading: PaddleOCR reads FDR Number, Principal Amount, IFSC & Expiry in 2 seconds without manual typing.
  • Photoshop & Tamper Shield: Error Level Analysis (ELA) detects edited fonts, modified numbers, or Canva fake receipts instantly.
  • Tri-Modal Bank Gateway: Works for ALL banks: Direct CBS API for Tier-1 banks, and a simple 2FA OTP Magic Link for rural co-op branches.
  • Autonomous SLA Engine: Daily chronometer bot sends proactive alerts on WhatsApp & email so files never get stuck.
The Game-Changer Impact
🚀
  • Zero Physical Couriers: Saves 50,000+ registered postal letters annually. 100% digital paperless audit trail.
  • Under 7-Day Refund SLA: Genuine institutions get deposits released quickly instead of waiting 6 months across bureaucracy.
  • Impossible to Tamper: Cryptographic hash ledger prevents even database admins from quietly editing records.
  • 100% Nationwide Bank Reach: Nariman Point SBI branch ho ya rural Bihar Gramin bank, every branch manager can verify with 1 tap.
